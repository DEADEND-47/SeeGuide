Important Safety Notice

SeeGuide is a supplementary aid. It NEVER replaces a white cane, guide dog, or human judgment.

Explicit Out-of-Scope Hazards:

  • Stairs, curbs, drop-offs, and potholes (single camera cannot detect negative ground depth)
  • Traffic signals, walk signs, and street-crossing decisions
  • High-speed vehicular traffic collision avoidance

Always use your cane or dog for ground tactile feedback.
